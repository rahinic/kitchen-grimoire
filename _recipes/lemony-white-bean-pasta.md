---
title: "Lemony White Bean Pasta"
tagline: "aglio e olio's cosier cousin"
date: 2026-10-25
category: dinner
serves: 2
time: 20
closing: "Simple ingredients. Big comfort."
leaflet: /assets/recipes/lemony-white-bean-pasta/illustrated.jpg
leaflet_plain: /assets/recipes/lemony-white-bean-pasta/plain.jpg
ingredients:
  - 200 g spaghetti
  - 1 can white beans, drained and rinsed
  - 4 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ tsp chilli flakes
  - 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - 1 lemon (zest + juice)
  - A handful of parsley, chopped
  - Salt & black pepper
  - Parmesan or nutritional yeast, to finish
steps:
  - Boil well-salted water. Cook the pasta 1 minute less than the packet says. Save a mug of the water.
  - Thinly slice the garlic. Chop the parsley and rinse the beans.
  - Zest the lemon, then squeeze its juice.
  - Warm the oil on low. Add garlic and chilli. Sizzle until pale gold, about 2 minutes. Don't brown it.
  - Add the beans and toss for 3–4 minutes, crushing a few with the back of a spoon.
  - Add the drained pasta and toss to coat it in the garlicky oil.
  - Add a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy.
  - Off the heat, stir in lemon zest and juice, parsley, and plenty of black pepper. Finish with Parmesan or nutritional yeast.
